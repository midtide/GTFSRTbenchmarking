    Data & methods

    Data

    The browser reads King County Metro's archived trip_updates and vehicle_positions Parquet files and the archived GTFS schedule directly from the public gtfs-realtime-archiver bucket behind gtfsrt.io, using HTTP range requests. Nothing is uploaded and this site never sees the data. Each archived file holds one UTC day, with one row group per poll (about every 20 seconds). A service day reads two UTC files, because evening service runs past midnight UTC.

    Finished days are saved in this browser (IndexedDB), so the next visit, or a different time-of-day window, reuses them without downloading again. The schedule in force is the most recent archived version captured by the end of that day.

    Actual departures

    Actual times come from vehicle positions. A trip has departed stop s when its vehicle is first reported with a current stop sequence greater than s. The departure time is the midpoint between that report and the one before it, and is used only when the two reports are at most 2 minutes apart (KCM vehicles report about every 24 seconds). The final stop of a trip has no departure, so it is not scored for accuracy. Departures are compared with each prediction's departure_time. KCM publishes equal arrival and departure times, and the departure is what a waiting rider can miss.

    ETA accuracy

    Follows the ETA Accuracy Benchmark. Each sampled prediction goes into a bucket by the time from when it was published (feed_timestamp) to the actual departure. It is accurate when actual − predicted falls inside that bucket's window, boundaries included:

    BucketEarliestLatest
    0–3 min30 s early1.5 min late
    3–6 min1 min early2.5 min late
    6–10 min1 min early3.5 min late
    10–15 min1.5 min early4.5 min late

    Overall accuracy is the straight average of the four bucket percentages, shown only when all four buckets have data. Predictions are sampled from every Nth poll; the benchmark allows a representative sample. Checked against every poll for one hour, 2-minute sampling matched within 0.6 points per bucket. Time of day is the hour of the actual departure.

    The bucket chart also shows, for predictions outside the window, how far off they were on average: the mean of |actual − predicted| over the too-early predictions, and separately over the too-late ones.

    Map

    Route shapes come from the archived schedule (shapes, cut at each stop with shape_dist_traveled). Each stretch between two stops is colored by the share of accurate predictions for departures from the stop where it starts, pooled across the four buckets, because single stops rarely have enough predictions in every bucket for the four-bucket average. Stretches with fewer than 20 scored predictions are drawn dashed and uncolored. Where routes share a street, the lines overlap; select a route to see it alone. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    ETA completeness

    Follows the ETA Completeness Benchmark. Every scheduled trip-stop (from stop_times, for services active that day) is complete when either:

    • the trip had an assigned vehicle (a vehicle position for the trip, or a vehicle_id in its trip update) and at least one sampled prediction for the stop was 0–15 minutes out, or
    • the trip was CANCELED or the stop was SKIPPED in the trip updates feed.

    Everything else is incomplete. The table splits that into trips with no vehicle and trips with a vehicle but no 0–15 minute prediction for the stop. Time of day is the hour of the scheduled departure. Ratings: ≥ 95% best in class, 90–94% good, 85–89% fair, below 85% needs improvement. With sparser sampling a very brief prediction window can be missed, so completeness can read slightly low at 5-minute sampling.

    Scope

    KCM's schedule bundle also includes Sound Transit, Seattle Streetcar and other routes. The table shows routes that appear in KCM's realtime feed in the selected days; routes in the schedule that never appear in the feed are counted in a note but left out. Realtime trip IDs that are not in the schedule are left out and counted in each day's notes.
